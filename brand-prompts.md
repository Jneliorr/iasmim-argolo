## Logo completo

Use case: background-extraction / restoration of an existing logo.
Edit target: the attached small logo for the nutritionist Iasmin Argolo.
Create a clean, high resolution restoration of THIS logo on a genuinely transparent background. Preserve the recognizable pineapple centered above and subtly behind the handwritten signature, with the professional descriptor below. Remove the entire cream/gray paper background and all faint decorative fruit patterns. Keep only the pineapple, signature, and descriptor. Improve the clarity of lines and edges; reproduce the original handwritten signature as faithfully as possible, with the EXACT name "Iasmin Argolo". The descriptor must be EXACTLY "NUTRICIONISTA" in small, spaced uppercase sans serif lettering. No other text. Pineapple crown olive green, stylized pineapple body fine muted gold lines. Signature dark forest green #314638 for strong readability, descriptor olive green #7c855b. Refined, simple flat artwork, no shadows, no embossing, no glow, no mockup, no background color. Preserve the overall stacked arrangement and delicate original personality; do not invent a different brand. Tight horizontal composition approximately 2:1 with a modest transparent safety margin, large enough to read clearly on a website. Actual alpha transparency required.
